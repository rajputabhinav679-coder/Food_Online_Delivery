GMAIL EMAIL SENDING SETUP
-------------------------
Order email notifications are configured to use:
  Sender:    abhinavsinghh0019@gmail.com
  Owner inbox for new orders: abhinavsinghh0019@gmail.com

To actually send emails, you must add a Google App Password:
1. Sign in to the Google account above.
2. Ensure 2-Step Verification is enabled.
3. Create a Google App Password for this app.
4. Open .streamlit/secrets.toml.
5. Paste the generated 16-character App Password into [email].app_password.
   Example: app_password = "abcd efgh ijkl mnop"
6. Save and restart Streamlit. On Streamlit Cloud, update App -> Settings ->
   Secrets and reboot the app.

Never use your normal Gmail password in app_password. Never commit
.streamlit/secrets.toml to a public GitHub repository.

NOTIFICATION FLOW
-----------------
1. When a customer places an order, the Cafe Owner inbox above receives an
   order notification, including customer details, food items, amount and
   delivery address.
2. When the Cafe Owner changes the order status from PLACED to CONFIRMED,
   all active Delivery Boy accounts that have a valid registered email receive
   the confirmed-order notification.
3. The first delivery boy who accepts the order in the Delivery Boy Dashboard
   becomes assigned to it.
4. No SMS or WhatsApp is sent. The customer phone number is included in the
   delivery email so the delivery boy can contact the customer.

REGISTER DELIVERY BOYS
----------------------
Use Cafe Owner Dashboard -> Delivery Boy Management. Register each delivery
boy with the correct active email address and mobile number. The email address
is the notification destination. The mobile number is included in the email.

3. Open Cafe Owner Dashboard -> Order Email Notifications (setup & log).
4. Confirm that the owner recipient is abhinavsinghh0019@gmail.com.

A SENT log entry means the SMTP server accepted the email. Check Inbox and
Spam/Junk to confirm final delivery. Actual email delivery cannot be verified
until a valid Google App Password is configured and a live test is sent.
